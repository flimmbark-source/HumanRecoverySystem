Build this complete workflow: scope one process → map it and observe the work → develop insights in freeform or bring in ideas developed elsewhere → connect evidence as it becomes relevant → compare options → test a small change in a scenario → export an anonymized decision brief.

Use actual work episodes to see where people contribute well, what enables them, and where conditions make the work unnecessarily difficult. Make these observations about activities in context. When an insight informs a recommendation, show the observations, the interpretation, and the gaps separately. Favor the smallest reversible improvement that strengthens how the organization already works. Show what it preserves, who gains or absorbs work, and how the team will check workload, work-life balance, and service quality.
